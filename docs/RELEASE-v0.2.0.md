# 動動學學 v0.2.0

給學齡前孩子的 Android TV 教具。這一版加了兩個新單元：刷牙計時和聽口令動身體。「聽聽看」也改得更公平。

## 新功能

- **刷牙小怪獸**：2 分鐘刷牙計時。牙齒分 6 區，每區 20 秒。刷完一區，那區的小怪就變成泡泡飛走。按 OK 開始後不用再按鍵。
- **巨人小螞蟻**：聽口令做動作。「變巨人」踮腳手舉高，「變小螞蟻」蹲下抱膝，聽到別的就停住。沒有跳的動作。每輪開頭都提醒大人陪在旁邊，連玩 3 輪會停下來休息。
- 首頁從 6 張卡變成 8 張。

## 修正

- **聽聽看：不能再靠眼熟猜答案。** 以前題目只出看過的卡，其他選項卻可能沒看過，孩子選眼熟那張就對。現在 3 張卡一樣眼熟，要聽音才答得出來。
- **聽聽看：新卡先示範再考。** 第 5、10 題先播新卡的音和例詞，再讓孩子選。每次最多教 2 張新卡。

## 沒變的事

- 離線、無廣告、不收集資料。
- 不計分、不打叉。新單元也不寫紀錄。

## 安裝

下載下面的 `learn-and-move-tv.apk`，照 README 的步驟側載。

# Learn & Move v0.2.0

An Android TV learning app for preschoolers. This release adds a brushing timer and a listen-and-move game. Listen and find is now fairer.

## New

- **Toothbrush monsters**: a 2-minute brushing timer. The mouth has 6 zones, 20 seconds each. When a zone is done, its monster turns into bubbles. Press OK to start. No more key presses are needed.
- **Giant and ant**: a listen-and-move game. "Giant" means tiptoe with arms up. "Ant" means squat and hug your knees. Any other word means freeze. There is no jumping. Each round starts with a reminder to keep an adult nearby. After 3 rounds, the app stops for a rest.
- The home screen now has 8 cards instead of 6.

## Fixed

- **Listen and find: no more guessing by familiarity.** Before, the answer was always a card the child had seen. The other cards were often new. Picking the familiar card was enough. Now all 3 cards are equally familiar. The child must listen.
- **Listen and find: new cards are shown first.** On questions 5 and 10, the app plays a new card's sound and word first. Then the child picks. At most 2 new cards per visit.

## Unchanged

- Offline, no ads, no data collection.
- No scores, no red marks. The new activities keep no records.

## Install

Download `learn-and-move-tv.apk` below. Sideload it with the steps in the README.
